Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

The Other Side Investigation Tracker does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

The extension stores evidence selections, clue selections, overlay mode, and overlay position locally in the viewer's browser using local storage. This data stays on the viewer's device and is used only to keep the overlay state between page reloads.

The extension does not use a backend server, analytics service, advertising tracker, or third-party data collection service.

When used on Twitch, Twitch may provide standard extension runtime information, such as channel context and viewer mode, through the Twitch Extension Helper. This extension uses that information only to run inside Twitch.

For privacy questions, contact the support email listed on the Twitch Extension page.
